A decade in luxury events, three ways to apply it.

Yana's career began in the events industry, where she spent close to a decade producing weddings and private and corporate events across Dubai, Miami, Los Angeles, and Milan. Along the way, she built a philosophy she now calls Impression Management — the belief that operational complexity should stay invisible, so the people in the room only ever feel the result.

That philosophy became the foundation for YD Event Management, her luxury event agency built on precision, trust, and creative fire. It later grew into Memories, a digital time-capsule platform for preserving what matters, and YD School, an online academy for the next generation of event professionals.

As demanding as this work is, Yana is endlessly grateful to her family and the people closest to her — for their support, and for believing in her every step of the way.

Yana Daryeva is a Dubai-based entrepreneur behind YD Event Management, Memories, and YD School — three ventures united by a single philosophy: Impression Management.
